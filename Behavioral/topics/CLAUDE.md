# Behavioral/topics

Index of the 4 numbered behavioral and leadership prep folders. Conceptual only: each holds one `NOTES.md` and its own CLAUDE.md.

## How to use (no build)
- Nothing to compile. The notes are SCAFFOLDS: stories, numbers and outcomes must come from your own real experience.
- Suggested order: `01` (STAR, story list, worked example, how the round is scored), `02`-`03` (people and engineering-leadership answer shapes), then `04` (story matrix, question bank, practice plan) to rehearse.
- Technical depth for stories comes from the other domains: incidents and debugging (`../../OS/code/72_PerformanceDebuggingMethodology`, `../../C_Basics/code/70_CombinedDebuggingCaseStudy`), design decisions (`../../SystemDesign/topics`).
- `02` and `03` end NOTES.md with a "Senior interviewer Q&A" section; new topics take the next prefix (`05_...`).
