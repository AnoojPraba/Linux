# 04_QuestionBankAndStoryMatrix

Practice harness: a story-to-theme matrix, answer pacing, a categorised behavioral question bank, follow-up probes, red flags, questions to ask and a 1-2 week practice plan.

## Files
- `NOTES.md` - (112 lines) sections: How to build your story matrix; Answer structure and pacing; Question bank by theme (with what is being tested); Follow-up probes interviewers use (practice answering each); Red flags to avoid; Questions to ask them (shows seniority); Practice plan (1-2 weeks); Mini example skeleton ("pushing back on a deadline") - illustrative numbers; replace with YOUR facts

## How to use this note
- Fill in the story matrix with 6-8 real stories and check every theme (conflict, failure, influence, technical depth, ambiguity, customer, deadline, mentoring) is covered by at least two.
- Follow the practice plan: record yourself, drill the follow-up probes, and prune red flags (blame, "we" only, no result).
- Replace the mini deadline-pushback skeleton's illustrative numbers with your own facts.
- Notes are scaffolds: the stories, numbers and outcomes must come from your own real experience; rewrite every example in your voice before an interview.

## Key trade-offs / interview angles
- Pacing: Situation about 15%, Task 10%, Action 50-60%, Result plus reflection about 20%.
- Question themes: ownership and impact, technical leadership, conflict and influence, failure and growth, people, ambiguity and delivery, customer/business/ethics, motivation and fit.
- Follow-up probes decide the interview; practise answering each.
- Prepare thoughtful questions to ask them (shows seniority).

- Scaffolds only: the skeleton uses illustrative numbers; every story must be yours.
