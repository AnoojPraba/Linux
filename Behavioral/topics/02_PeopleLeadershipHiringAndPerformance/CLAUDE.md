# 02_PeopleLeadershipHiringAndPerformance

People-leadership scaffolds: hiring, onboarding and delegation, performance management, conflict, leading change and team health, with a senior Q&A of prompts and answer shapes.

## Files
- `NOTES.md` - (129 lines) sections: What the interviewer is scoring; 1. Hiring; 2. Onboarding and growing people; 3. Performance management (underperformer story); 4. Conflict and difficult stakeholders; 5. Leading through change and ambiguity; 6. Team health and culture; Senior interviewer Q&A (prompts and answer shape)

## How to use this note
- For each section write one story from your own career (an underperformer, a hiring decision, a conflict, a reorg or migration) and map it to the answer shape in the Q&A.
- Rehearse the "Senior interviewer Q&A": managing underperformers, hiring decisions, difficult feedback, growing seniors, peer conflict, motivation on unglamorous work, brilliant jerks, managing up and across.
- Notes are scaffolds: the stories, numbers and outcomes must come from your own real experience; rewrite every example in your voice before an interview.

## Key trade-offs / interview angles
- Hiring: define the role and level rubric first, consistent questions, probe for signal over trivia, cite times you raised the bar.
- Delegation ladder: tell, sell, consult, agree, advise, inquire.
- Performance: clear expectations, early specific feedback, support plan, documented outcome; own your part when someone fails.
- Conflict: hear each side separately and find underlying interests; with product/leadership surface trade-offs (scope, time, quality); own it quickly when you were wrong.
- Leading change and ambiguity; team health and culture signals.
